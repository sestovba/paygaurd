Atmosphere

.orb-1 .orb-2 .orb-3 · .grid-lines · body::before

Three fixed radial-gradient orbs drifting on 14/18/20-second loops, an 80px grid, and an SVG turbulence noise film — all masked to fade out below the fold. You are looking at it right now: it is the background of this page. It costs nothing in markup and it is the single biggest reason the layout reads as expensive rather than as a form.

Nothing to render in a box — scroll and watch the top-right violet bloom move against the grid. Turn it off and every card below looks like a spreadsheet.

Three animated 600px gradients repaint continuously — battery and jank on an old WebView. Keep the look, kill the motion: bake the orbs into one static background image.

Inter 400–900 · JetBrains Mono 500/700

Two families with one job each: Inter carries the words, JetBrains Mono carries every number. That split is the whole typographic idea and it is worth keeping — money in a tabular mono is legible at a glance and never shifts width as it counts.

Month grid

.months-grid · .month-btn · .month-amount.safe|warning|over|empty

Six across, dropping to three then two. Each cell is name, badge, amount — and the amount's colour is the whole verdict, so twelve months of status read in one pass with no legend. Hover lifts 2px and floods the gradient in at 4%.

Read the states left to right: safe, safe, close, over, current (bordered violet), empty. Hover any cell.

Flat fills and a 1px border. Ports cleanly.Hover shadow is a 20px blur — drop it, keep the 2px lift.A twelve-cell grid is exactly what focusMode exists to remove. Beautiful, and against the house rule unless the reader asks for the year.

Buttons

.btn · .btn-primary · .btn-ghost

Two only. Primary is the brand gradient with a lift on hover and a 2% squash on press; ghost is a hairline that fills faintly. Two is the right number — nothing on the page has to guess which is the real action.

Primary carries a 20px violet blur that grows to 24px on hover. Replace with a 1px darker bottom edge.The lift-and-squash timing is the trait. Keep the curve, lose the shadow.Small variants are inline styles, not classes. Needs a .btn-sm.

Entrance

.reveal · .delay-1 … .delay-6

16px rise and a fade, staggered in 50ms steps down the page. Six delay classes, no JS, no observer. It is why the page feels assembled rather than dumped — and every block in this catalog is using it.

Reload the page and watch the top three sections arrive in order. Cost: two rules and six one-line classes.

Transform and opacity only — the two properties that stay on the compositor.Fires on every element regardless of prefers-reduced-motion. Needs the guard.
