Full case — problem, ownership, decision, trade-off

Business problem

Unclear order status on mobile drove support volume. Brand and CX needed consistency without slowing engineering handoffs.

Ownership

Senior UX via Axelon on att.com order management. I owned responsive design, a Figma sub-system (components, auto layout, tokens), HTML/CSS for states, and CX partnership on friction ranking.

The decision

Rank friction with CX by user impact × build cost. Fix the highest-leverage breaks first. Motion only where status changed — never decoration.

Trade-off

Ranking by leverage meant visible, frequently-reported friction stayed unfixed while lower-noise breaks that cost more users went first. That needed defending to stakeholders more than once.

Outcome

Full case — problem, ownership, decision, trade-off

Business problem

Onboarding burned time in redundant wizard steps while every compliance field still had to ship. Task completion and CSAT suffered; support absorbed the gaps.

Ownership

Senior UX via TEKsystems. I owned research synthesis, the ranked UI backlog, account-settings wizards, multi-step responsive views, and usability testing. Partnered with an external studio on key screens.

The decision

Collapse redundant steps. Show only the fields the current task needs. Live rate edits feed a broker preview so mistakes surface before review.

Trade-off

Nothing regulated could be removed — only resequenced. Every cut had to survive audit, so the flow got shorter in perceived effort rather than in required fields.

Outcome

+15% CSAT+10% task completion−30% wizard friction

Full case — problem, ownership, decision, trade-off

Business problem

Faith communities needed a focused place for prayer requests with real moderation — not another generic feed. Trust and roles had to exist before growth rhetoric.

Ownership

Sole designer-builder: information architecture, visual and interaction design, React UI, Firebase auth, RBAC, moderation tooling, and 20+ release cycles from usability feedback.

The decision

Ship moderation and roles in the first credible loop. A prayer wall without trust tools is just a guestbook.

Trade-off

Building trust tooling first delayed everything that would have grown the audience. The product is sound and small — that was the choice, not an accident.
