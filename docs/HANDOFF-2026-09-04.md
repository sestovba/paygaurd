## The finding that matters most

**The two prototypes are the two phases of the product, built separately by
accident.**

- The tracker has no jobs, no types, no ledger. A month is a bucket. It asks
  *what should I apply for?* → **Phase 1, Deciding**
- PayGuard has jobs, types, entries, a ledger, trial months. It asks *am I
  about to go over?* → **Phase 2, Recording**

That is exactly the two-phase model in `CLAUDE.md`, drawn by two people who had
not read it. They share almost no surfaces, which is why merging their
stylesheets would produce a page that does both jobs badly.

**So the ninth layout is not a visual blend. It is one layout carrying both
questions, with the hinge between them:**

> Are you working right now, or looking for work? · I'm working · I'm looking

Only the first card swaps. Trial months, the extra-paycheck warning, month
cells and the nav are identical in both, because those questions apply whether
or not you have found the job yet.

Budget: **~1,180 lines**, against PayGuard's 1,627 for one phase. Most of the
saving is that nothing recomputes a benefits rule.

## The design system, in brief

Full version in `components.html`. It sits **between** the two authorities that
already exist and replaces neither:

| Question | Answered by |
|---|---|
| What do we call it? | `docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §1.1` |
| What colour is it? | `src/styles/palette.css` |
| How big, how heavy, what states? | **`components.html`** |
| What does the number mean? | `src/domain/` |

### The eight rules

1. **One control height, one control type size** — `--control-md` 44px, `--text-md` 15px.
2. **Weight goes up as you go in** — a section stays 1px however deep; the card being edited takes the 2px ring.
3. **An emphasis may not change layout** — rings drawn inside with `outline-offset:-2px`.
4. **A label may not look like a control** — carried by fill, not size.
5. **Colour is never the only carrier** — every month cell says *left* / *over* / *all of it* in words.
6. **Missing is not zero** — em dash is a real zero; italic words mean never told.
7. **Explain at the point of doubt, never in advance** — no tour, no help icons, no tooltip library.
8. **One thing moves** — the pulse on the current trial month is the only animation.

Plus the device rule from `CLAUDE.md`, which is not ours to break: no
`color-mix()`, no `backdrop-filter`, no web fonts, no blurred shadows. All four
fail *silently* rather than degrading. Verified zero of each in the page.

### Scales

`--text-xs/sm/md/lg/xl/2xl/3xl` = 12·13·15·17·20·26·34, `--space-2xs…2xl` =
4·6·8·12·16·20·28, `--control-sm/md` = 36·44, `--radius-sm/md/pill`.

**Nothing in a component is allowed a number of its own.** Zero raw
`font-size` literals remain in the component CSS.

Two values sit outside the scale on purpose and are marked as such:
`--text-input: 16px` (below 16, iOS zooms the page on focus) and
`--control-md: 44px` (smallest reliable thumb target).

### Vocabulary decisions made this session

- Income-source chips are **Part time** and **Gig work** — not Employer/Gig, not W-2/1099. Named for what the reader does. **Cost:** "Employer" quietly said which door you came in, and the mileage deduction only exists behind one of them — so the selector copy now carries it: *"Gig work takes miles off before anything counts."*
- Gig gets **its own hue**. In hermes it was brand violet, so "this is the app" and "this is self-employment" were the same colour.
- Tabs are one word each: **Jobs · Pay · Limit**. A tab is a place, not a sentence.
- 26 copy shapes lifted from hermes, words replaced. **The shapes are the best writing in either prototype and the words are mostly on the banned list.** The unifying pattern: *the rule is stated where the number is* — never in a tooltip or a help page.

## Bugs found in the prototypes

Full detail in the two audit pages. The ones that generalise:

**PayGuard**
- The gauge divides a **year** of earnings by a **monthly** limit — pins to full by February.
- `countTwpUsed()` counts any month with any entry. No earnings threshold, no 60-month window despite the comment. `Math.min(…, 9)` means "all nine used" can be reached but never passed.
- `jobYTD` filters `e.year <= todayMonth().year` — every prior year included, labelled YTD.
- `.job-badge.1099` is an **invalid selector** (a class cannot start with a digit) and it invalidates the whole comma rule, so gig badges render with no colour at all.
- `el()` passes camelCase keys to `setProperty`, which only takes kebab-case — **19 style blocks silently dropped**, including the ledger totals' bold and mono.
- `textInput` destructures `{ placeholder, mono, required, id }` — **no `label`** — so the entry dialog rendered four unlabelled fields, two of them an identical `$0.00`.
- `<select>` given `value` as an attribute before its options exist — **every job displayed the first option**, so every job read "W-2" and "Weekly" whatever was stored.
- `footer.previousElementSibling` is null because `openModal` double-wraps the footer — **Add Entry and Save never worked at all.**

**Tracker**
- The year is stamped once on first run and **never advances**.
- "Hours I could work" divides the **full** limit, ignoring what is already counted — on the 28th with $1,100 counted it still answers 56 hours.
- Logs are push-only; the only correction is "Clear all".

**Both:** small text throughout (10–11px), no focus rings, no `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Corrections the previous session made to its own work

Recorded so they are not re-derived:

- `.job-card.active` was reported as a phantom state. **Wrong** — it is applied on line 917; the grep missed `' active'` with a leading space. It is the expanded-card ring and it is good.
- The add-card's right-hand text was catalogued as a keyboard shortcut `N`. **Wrong** — it reads `or click + above`, which is a much better thing: it names its own duplicate so the duplicate stops being one.
- The ledger specimen in `components-payguard-app.html` is **not verbatim** and now says so. The real ledger is 20 columns with 12 months inline; the specimen shows 6.
- The keyboard hint was shrunk from 16px to the 11px the author intended. **Wrong call** — the accident was better than the intent, and 11px is the size this whole audit keeps flagging as too small for this reader.
